Worth Running
As many of you must have already seen, we're currently in a massive recomp/decomp AI boom. Its positives and negatives have already been debated to death all around the internet.



For me personally though, an issue I've noticed is that websites focused on those areas tend to be complete polar opposites. They either allow visibility for rushed, fully vibe-coded projects that tend to have... questionable quality, to say the least, many times performing worse than emulation itself, or they completely deny AI releases altogether.



That has its merit, in my opinion, and it's a good thing those places exist since from the very beginning this whole scene started through human hands. However, I ended up wishing there could be some balance somewhere, where developers are allowed to use the technology as long as the end product is, well...decently made.



So the goal here is to post anything that might actually be worth your time, some points as always might be debatable, but i'll do my own best to point each out when possible, and if i don't: just let me know! so whether it's because of improvements, performance, features, or anything else, as long as it is fairly better than the other available counterparts in some shape, it will be posted here after proper testing.



I'll also add an donation option below, which is completely optional, as i'm doing this entirely but my own volition.
